# MAINTSUPP keyword-to-page map

Research date: 19 September 2026. Audience: UK commercial operators with **five or more sites**, across sectors. MAINTSUPP coordinates independent contractors; it is not presented as an employer of engineers, a software-only vendor or a provider of every FM service.

## Evidence and limits

This is a qualitative search-intent and competitor-page assessment, not a paid keyword-volume export. No monthly volume, CPC or numerical keyword-difficulty figures were available or invented. Difficulty below is an editorial estimate based on intent breadth and the competing pages observed, not a probability of ranking. Low competition can also mean low demand. Reassess against actual Search Console query and landing-page data after publication.

Searches sampled: UK multi-site maintenance and retail planned maintenance; UK maintenance coordination; outsourced commercial FM and compliance administration; retail kiosk maintenance; gym building maintenance; clinic premises maintenance; maintenance reporting and CAFM alternatives; commercial maintenance in the six proposed cities. Results vary by search engine, location and date; this is not a rank-tracking report.

The existing Search Console overview showed seven search clicks and one indexed page at review time. That is too little evidence to infer demand for an entire content estate. The canonical/title fixes subsequently landed in PR #60 and are preserved in this release; schema does not guarantee indexing.

## What the research changes

- Prioritise the multi-site commercial proposition. Unqualified **maintenance coordination** also surfaces residential letting-agent services and job searches. Add commercial, portfolio and multi-site language to distinguish buyer intent. [PMCS](https://maintenancecoordination.co.uk/) illustrates the residential overlap; [WH Integrated Solutions](https://whintegratedsolutions.co.uk/services/) uses helpdesk and maintenance-coordination terminology.
- Retail, reactive maintenance and PPM have recognisable supplier-search intent. [Proactive](https://proactivecommercialmaintenance.co.uk/sectors/retail/), [CSM](https://central-shopmaintenance.co.uk/nationwide-retail-shopfitting-maintenance/), [Sigma](https://sigmagrp.co.uk/services/project-solutions/planned-maintenance/) and [KTFM](https://ktfml.co.uk/services/multi-site-facilities-management) support these clusters. They also show established competition, so narrower intent is preferable to a generic “facilities management” homepage.
- Broad retail FM includes cleaning, security and waste services. [Mitie's retail offer](https://www.mitie.com/all-sectors/retail-shopping-centres/) illustrates that wider scope. Do not promise those services merely to match a keyword.
- **Gym maintenance** often means equipment servicing, as on [Fix Gym](https://fixgym.co.uk/). Target building/facilities coordination instead; [Tempus](https://tempusfm.co.uk/) provides a relevant gym-estate example. For clinics, [Surgery Premises Group](https://www.surgerypremisesgroup.co.uk/services) demonstrates premises rather than clinical-care intent. Do not imply medical-equipment servicing or specialist healthcare certification without evidence.
- Shopping-centre results include landlords' whole-centre plant and kiosk-leasing businesses. Separate tenant kiosk maintenance from these intents; compare [JCW core and shell](https://www.jcwes.com/shopping-centres-core-and-shell) and [SpaceandPeople kiosks](https://www.spaceandpeople.co.uk/solutions/pop-retail-shopping-centres/).
- **CAFM alternative** is substantially software/comparison intent. [The Digital Marketplace facilities-management listing](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/586358115177522) illustrates the software category. Explain the distinction between buying software and buying coordination; do not label MAINTSUPP a like-for-like CAFM replacement.
- City searches compete with established local or national operators, including [SIGUV in London](https://siguv.uk/) and [Cambridge Maintenance's coverage pages](https://www.cambridgemaintenance.co.uk/coverage-area/). A national target market does not prove contractor depth in every city.

## Seed decisions and cannibalisation checks

- `facilities management`, `facilities management UK`: broad, high competition and frequently broader service scope than MAINTSUPP. Context only, not primary targets.
- `multi site facilities management`, `outsourced facilities management small portfolio`: commercial variants feeding the homepage, but describe the actual coordination scope, not total FM.
- `reactive maintenance contractor UK`, `PPM provider UK`, `planned preventive maintenance provider`: service-page secondary research variants. Clarify the independent-contractor model; MAINTSUPP coordinates delivery.
- `shop maintenance company`, `retail maintenance company UK`, `facilities management for retail chains`: retail sector cluster, linking through to individual services. Do not optimise both homepage and retail page for the same retail-only title.
- `compliance management retail sites`: discuss retail context on the retail page, link to the compliance-administration owner; legal guidance needs primary-source review.
- `CAFM alternative`, `monday.com alternative for maintenance`: defer dedicated comparison pages. The reporting page may explain service versus software, but no software feature-equivalence promises. These terms are not primary targets in this launch plan.
- City pages must add genuine local evidence, distinct access/operational considerations and truthful availability. Do not generate six noun-swapped pages. Owner confirmation of national targeting is not city-level coverage confirmation.
- Branded utility queries are included for completeness, not treated as traffic-growth opportunities. Do not expand legal notices to satisfy a word count.

## Implementation and measurement

This release adds the keyword map, connected Organization/WebSite data and visible breadcrumbs to the five existing static non-homepage marketing pages and published CMS pages. The newly added CMS route /p/[slug] receives title-based breadcrumbs; individual CMS keyword ownership must be assigned when its content is known. It does not create the planned routes or alter paid campaigns. Schema describes facts; it does not guarantee a rich result or ranking increase.

For later pages: use one clear H1 and a distinct title matching the page's intent; self-referential canonical and social URL; original useful content; actual breadcrumbs; appropriate schema only where supported by content. Add a route to scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs only once it exists. Services should reference https://maintsupp.com/#organization as provider; articles should identify their real author and publisher. Maintain British English.

After release, review Search Console by query AND landing page at roughly four and eight weeks, then monthly. Track non-brand impressions, relevant clicks and enquiries from 5+ site portfolios. Two pages appearing for similar phrases is a signal to investigate intent overlap, not an automatic reason to delete either. Tracking changes are a separate batch.

Owner follow-up: supply official LinkedIn or other company profile URLs for sameAs; confirm city-level delivery coverage; obtain Sunnamusk publication permission and verified figures. No social profiles, accreditations, testimonials or trading premises have been invented.

# October 2026 update: London, one-off jobs and the 24-hour line

Research date: 4 October 2026. This section supersedes the audience line at the top of this file. The owner widened the proposition on that date:

- **Both buyers.** The monthly coordination plans stay as they are, for portfolios of five sites and above. Beside them, a single shop, kiosk, restaurant or office in London can book a one-off job, quoted before work starts.
- **Emergency call-outs at any hour**, for commercial premises in London. No arrival time is promised on any page.
- **Commercial premises only.** No page targets a domestic search.
- **Area order:** North London, then East, then West, then South. The three priority centres are Westfield Stratford City, Westfield London at White City, and Brent Cross. The owner confirmed that MAINTSUPP has coordinated real jobs inside all three, so those pages say so; no other centre is described as somewhere the company has worked.

MAINTSUPP is still described everywhere as a coordinator of vetted independent contractors. It is not an approved, nominated or authorised contractor of any centre and no page says so.

## Evidence and limits (October 2026)

Still no paid keyword-volume export: no monthly volume, CPC or difficulty figure was available or invented. Three kinds of evidence were used instead.

1. **Google autocomplete**, 99 seed phrases plus a deeper pass on 12. Autocomplete shows that a phrase is typed; it gives no volume, and an empty result means only that Google holds no stored completion.
2. **Competitor pages**, read for title, H1, headings, locations and proof: 15 kiosk firms, 20 strip-out and fit-out firms, 22 retail and commercial maintenance firms, and the ranking pages for each commercial trade.
3. **The live site**, read page by page before the change.

What that evidence changed:

- **Bare "kiosk" is polluted.** "kiosk installation", "kiosk removal" and "kiosk install and derig" return self-service terminals, kiosk-mode software, DVD kiosks and electrical enclosures. The kiosk page always says *retail* kiosk and names the shopping centre. "stall" and "RMU" are both typed ("westfield stratford stall hire", "rmu space in shopping centre") and both appear on it.
- **Nobody sells labour-only kiosk work in London.** Every kiosk-ranking competitor is a design-and-build manufacturer based outside London. No page found sells dismantling, removal or relocation of an existing retail kiosk. That is the clearest gap in the research.
- **Strip-out is searched by trade, not by "shop".** "strip out contractors london" and "retail strip out" are completed; "shop strip out london" is not. The title leads with the first.
- **"shop electrician" is a trap**: its completions are car and bike electricians. "commercial electrician london" and "retail electrician" are the clean terms. "commercial carpenter london" is rewritten by Google to "commercial carpentry london" and "commercial joinery london", so the page is named for those.
- **"shop repairs london" is consumer repair shops.** Not targeted.
- **No competitor has a landing page for a named London shopping centre.** The only centre-specific pages found are case studies and one Cardiff manufacturer's Westfield page.
- **No competitor title uses the word "coordination".** The homepage title no longer leads with the brand and that word.
- **Competitors sell against subcontracting** ("no subcontractors", "directly employed"). Every London page says plainly who does the work, because a buyer told up front is choosing accountability and one who finds out later is a complaint.

## Rules these pages are held to

`tests/seo-london-pages.test.mjs` enforces each of these.

- Title plus the template's suffix: at most 60 characters. Description: 110 to 160.
- Titles, H1s and descriptions are unique across the seventeen pages.
- Only the public telephone number appears anywhere under `app/(marketing)`.
- No "approved", "authorised", "nominated", "accredited" or "preferred" contractor claim.
- No promised arrival time.
- Every page says the work is done by independent contractors and who the page is for.
- A photograph's alt text describes the photograph, and never names a centre the picture is not of.
- Every internal link lands on a page that exists; the hub reaches all sixteen.

## Not done here, and why

- **No Google Business Profile, Search Console, directory or social changes.** They need the owner's own sign-in.
- **No `sameAs`.** The owner has LinkedIn, Facebook and TikTok pages; the URLs have not been supplied.
- **No prices for one-off jobs.** The owner chose "quoted per job". Competitors that publish an hourly rate and a call-out fee are named in the October 2026 research, and price words are heavily typed ("day rate london", "cost", "rates"), so this is the first thing to revisit.
- **No review or accreditation claims.** None has been supplied.
- **The enquiry form has no field for describing a job.** The London pages use a telephone link and an email link with a subject line instead. A "what do you need?" field needs a column and a migration.
